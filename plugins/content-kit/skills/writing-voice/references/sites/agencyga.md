# Site profile: agencyga.es

## Audience and goal

- Small local businesses (pymes, autónomos) in Donostia, Gipuzkoa and the rest of Spain; a few clients in the UK.
- Goal: win new local clients who need a fast, simple website, a better Google Business Profile, or local SEO. Every piece helps a business owner find and trust Agency-GA.
- Developer topics only with a clear business angle.

## Blog

- Categories, the existing set only: `seo-local`, `seo`, `diseno-web`, `desarrollo-web`, `pymes`, `autonomos`, `ia`, `accesibilidad-web`. Never create a new one. If the collection schema in the repo lists categories, the schema wins; report any difference.
- Before writing, check existing posts (the repo's blog collection, or https://agencyga.es/llms.txt) so a topic isn't duplicated. If an existing post covers the intent, suggest updating it instead.
- Commercial-intent queries ("mejor agencia SEO en Donostia", "qué agencia de diseño web me recomiendas en Gipuzkoa") belong on service pages, not blog posts.
- Internal links: only to pages that exist (check `src/pages` and the blog collection). Usual targets: `/servicios/diseno-web/`, `/servicios/posicionamiento-seo/`, related posts.
- End every post with a practical checklist or a clear next step.

## Google Business Profile

- Posts ("Novedad") promote the latest published blog post, for non-technical owners: problem → what it means for them → invitation to read.
- Max about 1,200 characters. No phone numbers. No hashtags. No exaggerated claims. Button "Más información" with the post URL.

## LinkedIn

- First person, slightly witty, no corporate filler. Strong first line (it shows before "ver más"). Short paragraphs. 3 to 5 hashtags. Max about 1,300 characters.
