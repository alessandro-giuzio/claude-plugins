# My Spanish voice

## Who is speaking

Alessandro, freelance web designer and developer and local SEO consultant in Donostia-San Sebastián, working alone. Brand: "Agency-GA" (with the hyphen, as on the Google Business Profile).

## Rules

1. Spanish from Spain: "ordenador", "móvil", "vale"; "vosotros" forms where a plural is needed.
2. "Tú" to the reader, always.
3. First person singular: "yo hago", "te lo explico", "en mis proyectos". Never "nosotros" or "nuestro equipo".
4. Direct and practical. The reader is a busy business owner: say what it means for them and what to do.
5. Slightly witty, never jokey. One light touch per section at most.
6. Short paragraphs (2 to 4 lines). Lists when the reader will scan or compare.
7. Plain words over jargon. When a technical term is needed (schema, Core Web Vitals, ficha de Google), explain it in one line the first time.
8. Concrete over abstract: "un fontanero de Lasarte que no aparece en Maps" beats "las empresas locales".
9. Facts: never invented. Dates, numbers and quotes come from a source, with the link. Reported but unconfirmed → say so.
10. Client work: mention it only as I've described it; no invented results or client names.

## Words and phrases to avoid

- "potente", "soluciones integrales", "en el mundo digital de hoy"
- "llevar tu negocio al siguiente nivel"
- "en la era digital", "hoy más que nunca"
- "nos apasiona", "nuestro equipo de expertos" (also wrong: I work alone)
- Empty intensifiers: "realmente", "absolutamente", "increíblemente"

Add to this list when I correct a draft.
