# Writing voice

Apply this voice and the right site profile to everything written for my sites and channels.

## Pick the site profile

- Profiles live in `references/sites/`. Use the one for the site the text is for: from the user's words, or from the repo being worked in.
- Available: `agencyga.md` (agencyga.es).
- No profile for the site: say so and ask before writing. Don't borrow another site's profile.

## Voice (Spanish)

Full rules and examples: `references/voice-es.md`. The essentials:

- Spanish from Spain. "Tú" to the reader. First person singular: "yo", never "nosotros". I work alone.
- Direct and practical, slightly witty. Write like explaining something to a client at a café, not like an agency brochure.
- No buzzwords: "potente", "soluciones integrales", "en el mundo digital de hoy", and the rest of the list in the reference.
- Short paragraphs. Concrete examples from real small-business situations over abstract claims.
- Never invent dates, numbers, quotes or client results. Anything reported but not officially confirmed is labelled that way. Every fact from the news has a source link.

## Lengths

Count characters for anything with a limit (titles, meta descriptions, social posts) and state the count. Don't estimate.
